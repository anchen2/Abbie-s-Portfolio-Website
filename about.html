Abbie Chen

UIUX Designer & Graphic Designer

I am a Graphic and UI/UX designer with 3+ years of experience using Human-centered design and storytelling to solve design problems and create connections between people and brands. From turning user journeys into organized, intuitive interfaces to using research to connect an audience to a brand, my design goals are rooted in empathy, functionality, sustainability and fun.

I’ve worked with clients including Mondelēz International FutureLabs @ UIUC and Calla Lily, applying existing brand guidelines to create a large-scale storytelling mural for Mondelēz and product signage for Calla Lily.

Additionally, as a team lead for Design for America, I partnered with a team of developers to translate an interactive prototype into a working app, keeping design intent intact from concept through build.

When I’m not designing, you’ll find me crocheting, playing video games, or spending time with family and friends

I’m currently open to full-time roles in Minnesota. Let’s connect on LinkedIn!
